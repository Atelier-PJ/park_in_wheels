# Vehicle transformation artwork

Built-in image generation was used for the watercolor concept sheet and its matching undecorated before sheet. Direction: preserve the same ivory family SUV, scene framing and storybook brushwork; reveal safari equipment, magical star trim, spaceship thrusters or submarine equipment. No labels are embedded in the art.

`python3 scripts/render-vehicle-gifs.py` (Pillow) crops the matching 2×2 sheets and renders a feathered top-to-bottom costume reveal with small sparkles. The four 732×480 GIFs run once for 4200 ms; matching WebP stills support reduced motion. Assets are bundled offline under `app/src/main/assets/vehicles/`.

This is artwork inside the app window, not modification of Pleos Driving view or physical vehicle equipment.
